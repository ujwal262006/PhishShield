Risk Methodology

PhishShield sums the points of each detected indicator, applies caps to avoid double-counting, and clamps the total to a 0–100 heuristic risk score.

Important: the score is a heuristic risk score out of 100, not a probability of maliciousness. A score of 82 means "82/100 heuristic risk", not "82% malicious".
