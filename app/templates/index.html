Security indicators we look for

  • Missing HTTPS
  • IP address as hostname
  • Suspicious keywords
  • Excessive subdomains & hyphens
  • Suspicious TLDs
  • "@" symbol & URL encoding
  • Punycode / IDN look-alikes
  • Possible typosquatting
  • Unusual URL structure
